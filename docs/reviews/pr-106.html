Private app captures. This revision is not installed on your desktop.

Complete or skip a habit

Previously, PLAY hid scheduled habits. You can now check them off or pass today.

  1. PLAY → HABITS. Click a habit. Look for its checkmark.
  2. Hold another habit or click its SKIP button. Look for SKIPPED.
  3. Reopen DailyXP. Both results remain saved.

Requires existing scheduled habits. Creating habits is outside this change.

Return to overdue work

Previously, PLAY hid overdue work. It now offers one small way back.

  1. PLAY → A SMALL WAY BACK. Find the one suggested commitment.
  2. Choose START SMALLER for a linked session of at most five minutes, TOMORROW to move it, or SKIP to pass.
  3. The suggestion disappears for today and stays gone after restart.

Appears when carryover work is overdue. Other overdue items stay unchanged.
