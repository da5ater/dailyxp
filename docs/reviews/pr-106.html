Create and check a habit

Previously, there was no way to create a habit. PLAY now offers NEW HABIT even when empty.

  1. PLAY → + NEW HABIT. Enter a name. All seven days are selected by default.
  2. Click SAVE HABIT, then click its chip on PLAY to complete it.
  3. Hold a chip, or click SKIP, to pass today. Reopening keeps the result.

Choose what happens to missed work

Previously, routines always let missed work go. You can now enable carryover on an existing or new routine.

  1. SETUP → your routine → CARRY MISSED WORK. Click OFF to switch it ON.
  2. Leave that routine unfinished. On the next DailyXP day, PLAY offers one A SMALL WAY BACK suggestion.
  3. Choose START SMALLER, TOMORROW, or SKIP. The strip clears for today.

Turning carryover on preserves missed work going forward. It does not recreate previously dismissed work or change your progress.
