1 · Plan and order

Previously, you could not organize elapsed parts of your day into ordered blocks.

Prepared: Learn and create (2 min), two 1-minute task targets, then Life and health (1 min). Neither block has started.

  1. In the actual DailyXP window, click Day blocks.
  2. Click Edit block on Learn and create. Try Move task down/up, then Save. No typing is needed.
  3. Try Move block down/up. Restore Learn and create first before starting.

2 · Task targets

Previously, focused Session time could not represent breaks and other elapsed day time.

Use the prepared first block after checking its order. Its clock counts all wall time.

  1. Click Start block on Learn and create.
  2. Wait one minute. You may visit Play; View day block brings you back when its reminder appears.
  3. Click Next task. Sketch an idea should become current while XP stays at zero.

3 · Next block

Previously, there was no manual handoff that gave each next block its own full duration.

Continue the same run; no extra setup or typing is required.

  1. At two minutes from the first Start, look for Start next block. Wait longer if you want to test the boundary.
  2. Click Start next block. Life and health should begin at 00:00 with its full minute.
  3. After that minute, click Finish plan. New day plan gives you a blank plan; work is not marked complete.
