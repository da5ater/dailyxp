Try the final DailyXP settings slice

The actual app is open on Settings with a separate REVIEW PROFILE. Choose one feature below.

Draft PR · Verification evidence

Settings that stay

Exports previously left your preferences behind. Motion also needed a separate Save click.

  1. In the actual DailyXP window, click Reduce motion. It saves immediately.
  2. Use DailyXP → Quit DailyXP. Open DailyXP again from your launcher.
  3. Click Settings. The toggle stays checked.

Own your JSON

The old export was a legacy storage backup and did not include preferences.

  1. In the actual app, open Settings and scroll to Your data.
  2. Click Export JSON. Keep the suggested file name and click Save.
  3. Expect Export saved. The file contains real progress and preferences.

Read the Session at a glance

Paused status used only a symbol, and XP feedback could briefly cover an active Session.

  1. Click Play, then Free Session. The bar shows running focused minutes.
  2. Right-click the DailyXP bar item. It shows Paused and the app offers Resume Session.
  3. Click Finish Session in the app. The idle bar returns to your level and rank.

Start fresh deliberately

Settings had no way to return a profile to the first-commitment state.

  1. Check the REVIEW PROFILE banner in the actual app. This is separate demo progress.
  2. Open Settings. Scroll to Start fresh and click Reset this profile.
  3. Cancel keeps everything. To try reset, click Clear this profile. Expect No commitments yet.
