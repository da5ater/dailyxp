# DailyXP

DailyXP turns intentional activity into durable progress, social competition,
and a recovery-oriented kingdom narrative.

## Language

**Goal**:
A long-term desired outcome that gives milestones and work their direction.

**Milestone**:
A measurable checkpoint toward a Goal.

**Task**:
A finite piece of work with a completion point.

**Routine**:
A recurring template that creates expected Tasks according to a schedule.
_Avoid_: Recurring Task

**Session**:
One timed period of focused work attached to a Goal, Milestone, or Task.
_Avoid_: Timer, Study block

**Habit**:
A repeated behavior recorded by completion or count rather than elapsed time.

**Recovery Track**:
A privacy-sensitive practice recorded through start, ongoing, and relapse
events rather than ordinary Habit completion.
_Avoid_: Sobriety Habit, Sobriety Counter

**Streak**:
An uninterrupted sequence of qualifying occurrences for a Habit or Recovery
Track.

**Achievement**:
Rule-triggered recognition that may award XP.

**XP**:
The permanent progress value awarded for qualifying DailyXP events.

**Momentum**:
A temporary progress state earned through recent consistency; unlike XP, it may
be lost through inactivity.

**Season**:
A bounded competition period with a start, end, membership, and ranking rule.

**League**:
A ranked cohort within a Season, scoped personally, privately, globally, by
country, or by region.

**Support Circle**:
An invitation-only group whose members may share Recovery Track progress and
send congratulations.
_Avoid_: Local Recovery League

**Group**:
A user-created competitive cohort with membership, discovery, and ranking
rules.

**Recovery XP**:
XP awarded within a Recovery Track for honest check-ins, milestones,
consistency, and returning after relapse.

**Rank**:
A user's position in a League, determined by Season XP.

**Story Rank**:
A narrative title reached at durable XP thresholds, from Wanderer through
Sovereign.
_Avoid_: Level name

**The Hollow King**:
The story antagonist who occupies parts of the user's kingdom when Momentum is
lost but cannot erase permanent XP or Achievements.

**Comeback Quest**:
A bounded sequence of intentional activity that restores Momentum and reclaims
occupied kingdom territory.
