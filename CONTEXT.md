# DailyXP

DailyXP turns intentional activity into durable personal progress, social
competition, and a recovery-oriented kingdom narrative.

## Intentional activity

**Goal**:
A long-term desired outcome that gives Milestones and work their direction.

**Milestone**:
A measurable checkpoint toward a Goal.

**Task**:
A finite piece of work with a completion point; it may stand alone or support a
Goal or Milestone.

**Routine**:
A recurring template that creates dated Task Occurrences according to a
schedule.
_Avoid_: Recurring Task

**Task Occurrence**:
One dated instance generated by a Routine.

**Session**:
One timed period of focused work, optionally attached to a Task and classified
by a primary Skill.
_Avoid_: Timer, Study block

**Habit**:
A repeated behavior recorded by completion or count rather than elapsed time.

**Daily Target**:
A person's intended amount of focused time for one DailyXP day.

**Planning Proposal**:
A previewable, editable set of potential commitments from a template or an
adaptive recommendation. Only acceptance changes the person's plan.
_Avoid_: Suggestion, Template (for the shared decision object)

**Day Boundary**:
The configurable local time at which one DailyXP day ends and the next begins.

**Skill**:
A hierarchical subject used to classify intentional activity, such as
`backend/study` or `philosophy/kant`.

## Recovery

**Recovery Track**:
A privacy-sensitive practice recorded through start, ongoing, and relapse
events rather than ordinary Habit completion.
_Avoid_: Sobriety Habit, Sobriety Counter

**Recovery Attempt**:
One uninterrupted period within a Recovery Track, ending only when a relapse
is explicitly recorded.

**Recovery Circle**:
A private group for sharing selected Recovery Track progress and supportive
congratulations.

**Recovery XP**:
A competitive progress value awarded for completed Recovery days and
milestones within the same Recovery category.

**Streak**:
An uninterrupted sequence of qualifying occurrences for a Habit or Recovery
Attempt.

## Progression and story

**Lifetime XP**:
The permanent progress value awarded for qualifying DailyXP events.
_Avoid_: Points

**Season XP**:
The resettable, standardized progress value used for competition during one
Season.

**XP Ledger**:
The explainable record of every Lifetime XP, Season XP, and Recovery XP award
or correction.

**Level**:
The unbounded numeric progression derived from Lifetime XP.

**Story Rank**:
A durable narrative title reached at Level thresholds, from Wanderer through
Sovereign.
_Avoid_: Level name

**Achievement**:
Rule-triggered recognition that may award Lifetime XP or a cosmetic reward.

**Momentum**:
A temporary state derived from recent consistency; unlike Lifetime XP, it may
rise or fall.

**Province**:
The part of a person's Reclaimed Kingdom that represents one Goal.

**Reclaimed Kingdom**:
The persistent story representation of a person's Goals and progress.

**The Hollow King**:
The story antagonist who may occupy inactive, unfinished territory when
Momentum is lost but cannot erase permanent progress.

**Comeback Quest**:
A bounded sequence of intentional activity that rebuilds Momentum and reclaims
occupied territory.

## Competition and community

**Season**:
A bounded competition period with a start, end, membership, and scoring rule.

**League**:
A ranked cohort within a Season, scoped globally, geographically, privately,
or by Skill.

**Division**:
A similarly active cohort within a League whose members may be promoted or
relegated after a Season.

**Rank**:
A person's position in a League, determined by its published scoring rule.

**Fixture**:
One round-bounded match between two players in a Head-to-Head League.

**Head-to-Head League**:
A League whose table awards three points for winning a Fixture, one for a draw,
and none for a loss.

**Group**:
A user-created cohort with explicit membership, discovery, privacy, capacity,
and competition rules.

**Support Circle**:
A private, noncompetitive Group for selected progress and congratulations.

**Guild**:
A persistent team that may compete against other Guilds using normalized
member contributions.

**Congratulations**:
A bounded supportive reaction to visible progress.
_Avoid_: Poke, Message

## Presentation

**Play**:
The primary surface for selecting work, running a Session, and recording the
current day's activity.

**Journey**:
The surface for progression, the Reclaimed Kingdom, Achievements, statistics,
and sharing.

**World**:
The surface for Leagues, Fixtures, Skills, Groups, Guilds, and discovery.

**Share Card**:
A user-reviewed image that presents selected DailyXP progress outside the
product.
